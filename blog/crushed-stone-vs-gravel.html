Crushed Stone vs Gravel: What's the Difference?

Published June 16, 2026 · Updated June 19, 2026 · GravelCalculator.ca

People use the words interchangeably, but there's a real difference: crushed stone is mechanically crushed with sharp, angular edges, while gravel is naturally weathered and rounded. That one difference changes how each compacts, drains and performs.

Compaction and stability

Angular crushed stone is the winner for anything structural. The sharp faces interlock when compacted, forming a firm surface that resists rutting. Round gravel can't lock the same way — the stones roll past each other, which is why pure rounded gravel makes a poor driving surface but a comfortable decorative cover.

Drainage

Both drain well, but in different ways. Clean (washed) crushed stone with no fines is excellent for French drains and behind retaining walls. Crusher run (crushed stone with stone dust) compacts tight and sheds water off the surface — ideal for a base layer rather than drainage. Rounded gravel drains freely but won't compact.

Which should you use?

  • Driveways & bases: crushed stone (crusher run base, clear stone top).
  • French drains & behind walls: clean angular crushed stone.
  • Decorative beds & borders: rounded gravel or river rock for looks and bare-foot comfort.
  • Pathways: either — crushed for firmness, rounded for appearance.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between crushed stone and gravel?

Crushed stone is quarried rock mechanically broken into angular pieces with sharp edges, while natural gravel is rounded by weathering and water. The angular shape makes crushed stone compact and lock together better.

Is crushed stone or gravel better for a driveway?

Crushed stone is better for driveways. Its angular edges interlock and compact into a firm surface, whereas rounded gravel shifts and ruts under tires.

Which is better for drainage, crushed stone or gravel?

Clean, washed crushed stone is the top choice for French drains and behind walls. Rounded gravel also drains well but won't compact; crusher run compacts tight and is better as a base than a drainage stone.

Is crushed stone cheaper than gravel?

Often, yes — basic crusher run and crushed stone are usually among the cheapest aggregates, while decorative natural gravel and river rock cost more per tonne.
