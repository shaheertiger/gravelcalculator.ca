Limestone vs Gravel: Which Should You Use?

Published June 22, 2026 · GravelCalculator.ca

Crushed limestone packs into a tight, hard, light-grey surface, while gravel drains better and resists weather. Here is how they compare and when to choose each.

When limestone wins

Crushed limestone is calcium carbonate, so it has angular faces and fines that bind into a firm, almost paved surface. Choose it for driveways and paths where you want a tight, clean, uniform finish that sheds water off a crown. The trade-off is dust and a tendency to hold some moisture.

When gravel wins

Granite or trap-rock gravel and washed clear stone drain faster and weather better, with less dust. Pick gravel for wet sites, drainage layers and French drains, or where you prefer a natural multi-colour look. Rounded river gravel is decorative only — it will not compact.

Frequently asked questions

Is limestone or gravel better for a driveway?

Crushed limestone compacts into a tight, hard, uniform surface, which many people prefer for driveways. Crushed gravel and clear stone drain better and produce less dust. For wet or poorly draining sites, gravel is often the safer choice.

What is the difference between limestone and gravel?

Limestone is a specific crushed rock (calcium carbonate) with angular faces and fines that pack hard but produce dust. Gravel is a broader category that includes crushed stone and rounded river stone, and washed clear stone drains better with less dust.

Does crushed limestone drain well?

Less well than clean clear stone. Limestone contains fines (dust) that fill the gaps and help it compact, but those fines also hold some moisture. For drainage layers and French drains, use washed clear stone instead.

Is limestone more expensive than gravel?

Prices are broadly similar and depend mostly on your region and how close the quarry is. In limestone-rich areas it can be the cheaper option; elsewhere local gravel may cost less. Get quotes for both delivered to your site.
