How to calculate cubic yards

A cubic yard is 27 cubic feet (3 ft × 3 ft × 3 ft). To find yardage for any material:

Cubic yards = (Length ft × Width ft × Depth ft) ÷ 27

Cubic feet → cubic yards: ft³ ÷ 27 (cf to cy)

Square yards → cubic yards: yd² × (depth ft ÷ 3)

Cubic yards → tons: yd³ × (density ÷ 0.842) for US tons, or × density × 0.765 ÷ 1.10 …use the calculator

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate cubic yards?

Multiply length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (ft), then divide by 27. For depth in inches, divide inches by 12 first. The calculator does this for any units.

How do I convert cubic feet to cubic yards?

Divide cubic feet by 27. For example, 54 ft³ ÷ 27 = 2 cubic yards.

How do I convert cubic yards to tons?

Multiply cubic yards by the material density. A cubic yard of gravel is roughly 1.1–1.4 tonnes; topsoil is about 1 tonne; sand about 1.2 tonnes. Enter a price per yard or tonne to also estimate cost.

How many cubic feet are in a cubic yard?

27 cubic feet equal one cubic yard (3 × 3 × 3 feet).
