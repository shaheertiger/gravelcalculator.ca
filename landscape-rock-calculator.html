How much landscape rock do I need?

Decorative landscape rock and stone is estimated like gravel: area × depth gives the volume, then multiply by density (≈1.5 t/m³ for most landscape rock, ≈1.44 t/m³ for river rock).

  • Decorative ground cover: 5 cm (2 in) over landscape fabric.
  • River rock beds / borders: 5–7.5 cm (2–3 in).
  • Larger feature stone: roughly the diameter of the stone.

Frequently asked questions

How much landscape rock do I need?

Multiply the bed area by the depth (usually 2–3 inches), then by the rock density (~1.5 t/m³). The calculator returns tonnes and cubic yards. Lay landscape fabric underneath to limit weeds and sinking.

How much does landscape rock weigh?

Most decorative landscape rock weighs about 1.5 tonnes per cubic metre; smooth river rock is lighter at about 1.44 t/m³.

How deep should decorative rock be?

About 2 inches (5 cm) for ground cover, or 2–3 inches for river rock beds and borders.

How much area does a tonne of landscape rock cover?

Roughly 13 m² (140 ft²) at a 2 inch depth.
