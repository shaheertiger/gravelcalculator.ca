How much limestone screenings do I need?

Screenings are the fine crushed limestone left after screening out larger stone (often 0–1/4"). They compact hard, so they are used as a bedding layer, not a base.

  • Paver or flagstone bedding: 1" (25 mm) over a compacted 3/4" crushed base.
  • Garden path or patio surface: 2–3" (50–75 mm), compacted in two passes.
  • Levelling a pool or shed pad: 2" (50 mm).

Tonnes = Area (m²) × Depth (m) × 1.6

Example

A 10 × 20 ft patio (200 ft²) with 1" of screenings: 200 ÷ 12 = 16.7 ft³ = 0.62 yd³ ≈ 0.75 tonnes. Order one tonne to allow for compaction and screeding.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a yard of limestone screenings weigh?

About 1.2 tonnes (roughly 2,700 lb) per cubic yard when dry; damp screenings weigh more.

How many square feet does a tonne of screenings cover?

About 265 ft² at 1 inch deep, 130 ft² at 2 inches, or 90 ft² at 3 inches.

How deep should screenings be under pavers?

About 1 inch (25 mm), screeded flat over a compacted crushed-stone base. Thicker bedding layers tend to shift.

Are limestone screenings the same as stone dust?

Yes, the terms are used interchangeably in Canada. Some yards also sell them as A-gravel fines or crusher dust.
