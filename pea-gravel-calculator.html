How much pea gravel do I need?

Pea gravel is small (≈6–10 mm), rounded stone ideal for patios, paths, play areas and drainage. To estimate it, multiply the area by the depth, then convert to weight using a density of about 1.79 t/m³ (≈3,015 lb/yd³).

  1. Measure the area to cover.
  2. Choose a depth — 2 inches (5 cm) is typical for pea gravel; use 3 inches for a softer, deeper layer.
  3. Calculate the volume, then the weight and number of bags.

How many bags of pea gravel?

Bagged pea gravel is often sold in 0.5 ft³ bags (about 50 lb). There are roughly 54 bags per cubic yard. For larger jobs, bulk delivery by the tonne or cubic yard is far cheaper.

Frequently asked questions

How much pea gravel do I need?

Multiply the area by the depth (commonly 2 inches) to get the volume, then multiply by 1.79 t/m³ for the weight. The calculator above does this and shows bags, tonnes and cubic yards.

How deep should pea gravel be?

About 2 inches (5 cm) for paths and decorative areas, or up to 3 inches for a softer surface or play area. Use landscape fabric underneath to limit sinking and weeds.

How many bags of pea gravel in a cubic yard?

Around 54 half-cubic-foot bags per cubic yard. Bulk delivery is cheaper for anything over about one cubic yard.

How much does pea gravel weigh?

Pea gravel weighs about 1.79 tonnes per cubic metre, or roughly 3,000 lb per cubic yard.
