River Rock Calculator

Find out how much river rock you need for garden beds, borders and dry creek beds. Enter the area and depth to get tonnes, cubic yards, bags and cost.

Quick answer

River rock weighs about 1.44 t/m³, so one cubic yard is about 1.1 tonnes. At a typical 2" layer, one tonne of river rock covers about 145 ft²; larger 3–5" rock needs 3–4" of depth to hide the fabric.

How deep should river rock be?

Set the depth by the size of the stone: the layer should be about twice the stone's diameter so no fabric shows through.

Coverage per tonne = 1 ÷ (1.44 t/m³ × depth in metres) m², converted to ft².

How many bags of river rock do I need?

Bagged river rock is usually sold in 0.5 ft³ bags (about 18–20 kg). A cubic yard is 27 ft³, so it takes about 54 bags per cubic yard. Beyond about half a yard, bulk delivery is almost always cheaper; see the bulk vs bagged calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How much area does a tonne of river rock cover?

About 145 ft² at 2 inches deep, 100 ft² at 3 inches, or 75 ft² at 4 inches.

How much does a yard of river rock weigh?

About 1.1 tonnes (roughly 2,400 lb) per cubic yard; denser granite river stone can reach 1.3 tonnes.

How deep should river rock be in a garden bed?

2 inches for small 3/4–1 inch stone, 3–4 inches for 1–3 inch rock, and 4 inches or more for larger cobble.

Should I put landscape fabric under river rock?

Usually yes, to slow weeds and stop the rock sinking into the soil; edge the bed so the rock stays put.
