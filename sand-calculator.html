How much sand do I need?

Sand is estimated by area × depth. Dry sand weighs about 1.6 t/m³ (wet sand is heavier, ≈1.92 t/m³). Common uses and depths:

  • Paver setting bed: 2.5 cm (1 in) of coarse sand over a compacted base.
  • Sandbox: fill to the desired play depth (often 20–30 cm / 8–12 in).
  • Bedding / levelling: 2.5–5 cm (1–2 in).

For patios and paths, sand is paired with a crushed-stone base — see the patio gravel calculator.

Bags of sand

Bagged sand is commonly 25 kg; there are roughly 60 bags per cubic yard. Bulk sand by the tonne is far cheaper for larger jobs.

Frequently asked questions

How much sand do I need?

Multiply the area by the depth. A 100 ft² paver bed at 1 inch needs about 0.3 cubic yards (≈0.6 tonnes) of sand. The calculator shows cubic yards, tonnes and bags.

How much does a cubic yard of sand weigh?

About 1.2–1.4 tonnes for dry sand, and more when wet (up to ~1.5 tonnes per cubic yard).

How much sand for a paver base?

A 1 inch (2.5 cm) coarse-sand setting bed over a compacted crushed-stone base. Calculate the base separately with the patio or crushed gravel calculator.

How many bags of sand in a cubic yard?

Roughly 60 bags of 25 kg each per cubic yard; bulk sand is cheaper for larger areas.
