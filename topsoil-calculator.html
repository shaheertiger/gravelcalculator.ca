How much topsoil do I need?

Topsoil is estimated by area × depth. Loose topsoil weighs about 1.3 t/m³ (≈1 tonne per cubic yard), though wet or compost-rich soil is heavier.

  • New lawn / overseeding: 5–10 cm (2–4 in).
  • Garden beds: 15–20 cm (6–8 in).
  • Raised beds: fill to the box depth (often 25–30 cm / 10–12 in).

Bags of topsoil

Bagged soil is often sold in 25 L bags — about 30 bags per cubic yard (≈40 bags per m³). Bulk delivery is much cheaper for beds and lawns.

Topsoil (yd³) = (Length ft × Width ft × Depth ft) ÷ 27

Frequently asked questions

How much topsoil do I need?

Multiply the area by the depth. For example, 100 ft² at 4 inches needs about 1.2 cubic yards. The calculator shows cubic yards, tonnes and bags.

How much does a cubic yard of topsoil weigh?

About 1 tonne (roughly 1,000–1,300 kg) for typical screened topsoil; wetter or compost-heavy soil weighs more.

How many bags of topsoil in a cubic yard?

About 30 bags of 25 litres each per cubic yard. Bulk delivery is cheaper for larger areas.

How deep should topsoil be for a new lawn?

About 2–4 inches (5–10 cm) of quality topsoil before seeding or sodding.
